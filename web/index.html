Common questions from overseas buyers
Do non-UK residents pay extra Stamp Duty?
Yes — a 2% surcharge on top of whatever rate already applies, if you're present in the UK fewer than 183 days in the 12 months before completion, on properties £40,000+.
Does it stack with the buy-to-let surcharge?
Yes. A non-resident buying an additional property pays +5% (additional property) and +2% (non-resident) on top of the standard bands — up to 19% on the top slice.
What counts as "non-resident" for this surcharge?
HMRC counts days physically present in the UK in the 12 months before completion — under 183 days and the surcharge applies, regardless of nationality or visa.
